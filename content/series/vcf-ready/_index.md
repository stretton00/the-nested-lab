A short series on one tool. VCF Ready reads a vSphere estate, from RVTools exports or through a vCenter account with read-only permissions, and checks it against cited Broadcom guidance for a move to VCF 9. The first post says what it reads and how it decides. The second is the plan: the route, the phases, the sizing, the blueprints and the sites and regions they span, from Broadcom's own workbook, planner and design pages. The third shows how an assessment leaves a customer's site without the customer in it. The fourth is about writing your own checks, run through vCenter, with every change warned about and approved. For consultants and platform teams who have been asked whether an estate will go. Read them in order.
